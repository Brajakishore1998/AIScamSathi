🚨 Cybercrime Emergency Help

If money has been lost or an account is at risk, act quickly.

India Cyber Fraud Helpline

1930

If you have lost money to cyber financial fraud in India, call 1930 promptly. Also report through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

📞 Call 1930

Official reporting portal

Use the official government portal. Keep transaction details and relevant evidence ready. Never share an OTP with anyone claiming to help.

cybercrime.gov.in ↗

What to do now

  1. Contact your bank or payment provider using its official app or number.
  2. Block compromised cards or accounts and change exposed passwords from a trusted device.
  3. Save transaction IDs, messages, call details and screenshots as evidence.
  4. Do not pay anyone who promises to recover your money for an advance fee.
